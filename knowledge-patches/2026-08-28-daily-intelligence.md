## 1. Australian online spending is favouring affordable discretionary treats

Source: Australia Post, July 2026 Quarterly eCommerce Update  
URL: https://auspost.com.au/content/dam/auspost_corp/media/documents/ecommerce-reports/quarterly-ecommerce-reports/july-2026-quarterly-ecommerce-update.pdf

Affected leads: Renee, Finlay, Clarity, Atlas

Operational effect: consider restrained, coherent gift add-ons and bundles where the client's catalogue and margin evidence support them. Do not infer that DFG will convert better, discount automatically, or promote marketplace dependence without product, margin and client evidence.
