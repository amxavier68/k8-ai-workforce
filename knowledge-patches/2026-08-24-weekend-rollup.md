## 1. ACCC — independent automotive repair information access
- Source: Australian Competition and Consumer Commission, 2026-08-21
- URL: https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/subaru-in-court-for-allegedly-failing-to-give-independent-repairers-equal-access-to-car-repair-information
- Status: Confirmed court proceeding and ACCC allegation; allegations are not findings.
- Verified change: The ACCC commenced its first court proceeding concerning alleged breaches of the Motor Vehicle Service and Repair Information Sharing Scheme. The scheme requires eligible service and repair information to be offered to Australian repairers on fair and reasonable commercial terms, including suitable subscription periods.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Sentinel, Clarity, Atlas
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: For automotive-tradie clients, distinguish access-to-information and compliance constraints from ordinary marketing underperformance. Preserve allegation/finding language and do not imply a client has scheme rights without checking eligibility and facts.
