## 1. Google Search Console — Generative AI performance reporting
- Source: Google Search Central, 2026-06-03
- URL: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/06/gen-ai-performance-reports
- Status: Confirmed limited rollout to a subset of sites.
- Verified change: Search Console now has dedicated reports for visibility in generative AI features on Search and Discover, including impressions, pages, countries, devices (Search), and dates.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Clarity, Atlas, Kai
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: Treat generative-search visibility as increasingly measurable where the report is available. Do not infer metrics for properties without access and do not invent separate KPI baselines until data exists.

## 2. Google Search Console — social/video platform properties globally available
- Source: Google Search Central, 2026-07-29
- URL: https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/07/platform-properties-social-video-guide
- Status: Confirmed global availability.
- Verified change: Search Console platform properties can track Google Search/Discover/News performance for Instagram, TikTok, X, and YouTube content.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Clarity, Atlas
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: Omni-channel authority evidence can include Google discovery performance for verified social/video properties, rather than treating social visibility and search visibility as fully separate evidence streams.

## 3. Shopify — WebMCP support on storefronts
- Source: Shopify Developer Changelog, 2026-08-05
- URL: https://shopify.dev/changelog/webmcp-liquid-hydrogen
- Status: Confirmed Shopify implementation; WebMCP remains an emerging proposed standard with limited current browser-agent support.
- Verified change: Liquid storefronts expose tools agents can call for catalogue search, product inspection, cart management, checkout, orders, policies and FAQs. Shopify states this is live on Liquid storefronts; agent support is currently limited.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Kai, DevMate, Atlas
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: Treat agent-callable commerce as a real platform direction, but not yet as a universal client requirement. Preserve separation between content/data/actions and interface layers in future architecture; do not oversell 'agent readiness'.

## 4. Australian eCommerce — delivery choice and smaller baskets
- Source: Australia Post eCommerce Report 2026
- URL: https://auspost.com.au/business/ecommerce/ecommerce-report
- Status: Confirmed report data based on 2025 Australian shopping behaviour.
- Verified change: Australia Post reports $82.6b online spend in 2025, average baskets down to $96, 82% of households shopping online, and 69% of shoppers wanting multiple delivery options at checkout.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Clarity, Finlay
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: Treat delivery clarity, cart value, cross-sell/free-shipping thresholds and checkout choice as evidence-backed Australian eCommerce UX considerations. No automatic paid-tool recommendation follows.
