## 1. Australian online product-safety reform signal

Source: Australian Competition and Consumer Commission, published 2026-09-01: https://www.accc.gov.au/media-release/stronger-product-safety-rules-needed-including-for-online-marketplaces

Affected leads: Renee, Sentinel, Clarity, Atlas

Verified change: The ACCC says Australia's current product-safety framework has gaps for online marketplaces and supports Treasury-led reform, including possible mandatory marketplace obligations, stronger injury reporting and penalties. This is a regulator position and reform process, not a new operative general product-safety law.

Operational effect: when advising ecommerce clients, distinguish current supplier duties, bans, standards, recalls and mandatory reports from proposed marketplace reform. Treat product provenance, safety information and recall pathways as protective friction. Do not present proposed reforms as enacted law.

## 2. Google Ads begins automatic AI Max transitions for selected legacy settings

Source: Google Ads, published 2026-04-15 and updated 2026-06-11: https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/dsa-upgrade-to-ai-max-2026/

Affected leads: Renee, Sammi, Finlay, Sentinel, Clarity, Atlas

Verified change: Starting in September 2026, eligible campaigns using Automatically Created Assets or campaign-level broad match begin automatic transition to AI Max. Google delayed Dynamic Search Ads sunset and auto-upgrade until February 2027. Google says accounts will receive notifications and legacy settings will be mapped during transition.

Operational effect: inspect the actual advertising account and change notice before advising. An automatic platform transition does not authorise K8 to enable, expand, spend, rewrite assets, change landing pages or accept broader query matching. Preserve a pre-change snapshot, compare post-change search terms/assets/URLs and retain Finlay's spend gate and owner approval.
