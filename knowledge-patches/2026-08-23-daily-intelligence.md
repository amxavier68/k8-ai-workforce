## 1. Australia Post — AI is already part of Australian shopping discovery, but agentic trust lags
- Source: Australia Post eCommerce Report 2026
- URL: https://auspost.com.au/business/ecommerce/ecommerce-report
- Status: Confirmed report data based on 2025 Australian shopper/business survey evidence.
- Verified change: Australia Post reports 62% of shoppers use AI, 32% already use AI for shopping advice, while 44% of surveyed businesses advocate agentic commerce compared with 16% of shoppers.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Clarity, Kai, Finlay, Atlas
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: Treat AI-assisted product discovery as present Australian behaviour, but do not assume shoppers are ready to delegate transactions to agents. Separate discovery readiness from autonomous-purchase readiness in client recommendations.

## 2. Australia Post — sale-event planning should protect trust and basket quality
- Source: Australia Post eCommerce Sale Event Calendar 2026
- URL: https://auspost.com.au/business/grow-your-business/business-ideas-blog/ecommerce-calendar
- Status: Confirmed current 2026 guidance.
- Verified change: Australia Post reports 73% of Australian shoppers wait for sale events and 81% shop around for the best deal; Father's Day is 6 September 2026 and Black Friday is 27 November 2026.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Clarity, Finlay
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: Event-based merchandising can be recommended when relevant, but K8 should prefer clear value, bundles, delivery promises and occasion relevance over habitual blanket discounting.

## 3. Google Search — Preferred Sources now has a first-party interactive publisher button
- Source: Google Search Central documentation, last updated 2026-08-20
- URL: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/preferred-sources
- Status: Confirmed global feature/documentation update.
- Verified change: Eligible domain/subdomain publishers can add Google's interactive Preferred Sources button or deeplink. User-selected sources may be highlighted in Top Stories and, where available, AI Mode and AI Overviews.
- Affected leads: Renee Archer, Clarity, Atlas, DevMate
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: For sites that publish fresh, recurring authority content, Preferred Sources becomes a legitimate audience-retention/discovery mechanism. It is not a ranking hack and should not be recommended to brochure sites with no publishing cadence.

## 4. Web platform — native form controls and accessibility continue to converge
- Source: web.dev, 'New to the web platform in July', published 2026-07-31
- URL: https://web.dev/blog/web-platform-07-2026
- Status: Confirmed stable/beta browser status as documented by web.dev.
- Verified change: Firefox 153 changed select parsing to permit richer descendants, moving customizable native select controls closer to cross-browser implementation; Safari 27 beta includes ariaNotify and other accessibility/platform improvements.
- Affected leads: DevMate, Renee Archer
- Classification: KNOWLEDGE PATCH
- Effect: Prefer progressive enhancement and native semantic controls where possible; do not replace accessible native selects with custom JavaScript merely for styling when platform support is still converging.
