## 1. Australian branded SMS sender verification

Source: business.gov.au / Australian Communications and Media Authority guidance, published 2026-05-08; requirement effective 2026-07-01  
URL: https://business.gov.au/news/new-rules-for-businesses-sending-text-messages

Affected leads: Renee, Sentinel, Sammi, Atlas

Change: Australian businesses using an alphanumeric branded SMS sender ID must register it through their messaging provider or messages may be labelled unverified. Messages sent from an ordinary phone number are outside this specific sender-ID requirement.

Operational effect: during CRM/SMS discovery, first verify whether a branded sender ID is actually used. Do not infer DFG uses SMS, register an ID, change credentials, or contact a provider without owner/client authority.

## 4. Shopify theme-editor provenance in Git commits

Source: Shopify Developer Changelog, 2026-08-26  
URL: https://shopify.dev/changelog

Affected leads: Renee, DevMate, Atlas
